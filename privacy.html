Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

“Yaqeen” (يَقين) is a YouTube channel that publishes short Islamic reminders: youtube.com/@yaqeen_qisas. “Yaqeen Publisher” is a private tool used only by the channel owner to schedule uploads of the channel’s own videos.

What data the app accesses

The app asks Google for permission to manage the owner’s YouTube account (the YouTube Data API “upload/manage videos” scope). It is used only to upload the channel’s own videos and set their title, description, tags and visibility. The app does not read, collect or store any data about viewers or any other Google user.

How the data is used and stored

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only inside the owner’s self-hosted automation server (n8n) on the owner’s own computer, protected by that server’s encryption.
  • Video files and their texts are prepared locally by the owner; nothing is sent to any third party other than Google/YouTube itself.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train models.

Google API Services

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it immediately stops the app from uploading anything.

Viewers

If you only watch the channel, this app never touches your data. Your activity on YouTube is governed by YouTube’s own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the channel’s YouTube page: youtube.com/@yaqeen_qisas/about.
